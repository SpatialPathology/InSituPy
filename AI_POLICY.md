# AI Policy

InSituPy welcomes AI-assisted contributions. This short policy is what keeps them safe to merge
with limited maintainer time. It applies to everything you submit: code, tests, documentation,
comments, and issue text.

## The one rule: you are responsible

The human who opens a pull request is fully responsible for it. You must understand every line you
submit and be able to explain and maintain it. "The AI wrote it" is never an explanation.

## Required before you open a pull request

1. **Personally review all AI-generated output** - code, tests, docs, comments - line by line.
2. **Run an AI-assisted review over your changes** (see [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md); the
   project provides a `/review` workflow for this) and **paste a short summary of what it found
   into your PR**: issues you fixed, and any you are knowingly leaving open.

## Not acceptable

- Raw, unreviewed AI output pasted into a PR.
- Bot- or agent-opened PRs, commits, or comments without a human reviewing and approving them.
- **Crediting an AI as author or co-author.** Do not add `Co-Authored-By: <AI>` trailers or
  "Generated by ..." / "Written by ..." lines to commits or PRs. You are the sole author of record
  and fully responsible. Disclosing that you *used* AI (the review summary above) is required; the
  AI is not an author.
- Contributions that cost maintainers more time to review than they add in value. Make clear why you think this contribution is important. And if AI made the decisions, let it explain why the changes were needed. "AI said it is better is not an explanation").

## Writing InSituPy code with AI

Point your assistant at the **`insitupy-api` skill** (easiest, no setup) or the **MCP server** (for
power users) so it stays current with the real API - see [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the
[README](README.md). Guessing at the API from memory is the most common source of broken AI output.

---

Maintainers may label a PR `needs-ai-review`, request changes, or close PRs that are clearly raw
AI output or that the contributor cannot explain. Human maintainer review always has the final say.
